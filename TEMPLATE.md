# app-template

The platform's paved-road template for internal tools. Repositories are created from it
by the platform scaffolder, which fills in the `__PLACEHOLDERS__` from the intake
manifest and registers the app in the catalog. Published from the platform repo at
`b48b255`; edit it there, not here.

Platform-owned files in every app (the workspace cannot change them): `app.yaml`,
`CLAUDE.md`, `Dockerfile`, `.github/`, `.devcontainer/`, `.githooks/`.
